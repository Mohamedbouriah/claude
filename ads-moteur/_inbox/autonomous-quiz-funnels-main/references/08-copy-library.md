# 08 — Copy Library

A reusable bank of high-converting copy patterns: headlines, sub-titles, CTAs, micro-insights, social proof, FAQ, loss aversion. Use as starting points and adapt to product specifics.

Provided in **English** and **French (vouvoiement off, anti-hype)**.

## Paywall CTAs (validated by lift)

### English (winners)

- **"Continue"** — beat descriptive CTAs by **+111%** (Stormy, 4500+ A/B tests)
- "Start free trial"
- "Try for $0.00" (Cal AI signature, "free" feels softer than "trial")

## Social proof patterns (validated)

### English (high-converting structure)

- **Quantitative**: "Used by [X+] [segment description] like you"
- **Quantitative**: "[X.X]★ · [Y]K+ reviews"
- **Quantitative**: "Join [X]M users"
- **Segmented testimonial**: "[Name], [age], [outcome]: [1-line quote]"
- **Trust mark**: "Featured in [NYT / WSJ / Forbes / TechCrunch]"
- **Specificity**: "12,847 plans created in the last 24h" (real number, not rounded)

### Star rating sweet-spot

- **4.2-4.5★ feels more authentic** than 5.0
- Adding review count multiplies trust: "4.7★ · 23,000 reviews" >> "4.7★"

### French (high-converting structure)

- **Quantitatif**: "Utilisé par [X+] [segment comme vous]"
- **Quantitatif**: "[X,X]★ · [Y]k+ avis"
- **Quantitatif**: "Rejoignez [X]M d'utilisateurs"
- **Témoignage segmenté**: "[Prénom], [âge], [résultat] : [phrase courte]"
- **Trust mark**: "Vu dans [Le Monde / Les Échos / BFM / Capital]"
- **Spécificité**: "12 847 plans créés ces dernières 24h"

## Anti-patterns to never write

- "Welcome to [App Name]!" (vacuous, gives no value)
- "Discover all our amazing features!" (features ≠ outcomes)
- "Don't miss out!" (generic FOMO)
- "We are excited to have you!" (the user is not excited about you)
- "Sign up now to get started" (creates friction without explaining value)
- "Best [X] in the world!" (unverifiable claim, kills trust)
- "Limited time offer!" with a fake countdown that resets

## Tone calibration by category

| Category | Tone | Avoid |
|---|---|---|
| Health & Fitness | Direct, supportive, data-grounded | Toxic positivity, body-shaming |
| Education | Encouraging, level-appropriate | Patronizing |
| Productivity | No-nonsense, time-focused | Hype, motivational fluff |
| AI tools | Capable, transparent on limits | Magical thinking, AGI claims |
| Finance | Cautious, transparent | Get-rich-quick, FOMO |
| Dating | Warm but realistic | Gendered stereotypes, manipulation tactics |
| Mental health | Gentle, non-clinical | Self-diagnosis prompts |
| Premium SaaS | Confident, simple | Buzzwords, jargon |
| Developer tools | Technical, precise | Marketing-speak, oversimplification |

## Universal copy checklist

Before shipping any screen, verify:

1. **One idea per screen.** No double-headline.
2. **Every claim is defensible.** No "scientifically proven" without source.
3. **Outcomes, not features.** "Get [result]" beats "Use [feature]".
4. **Active voice.** "We adapt your plan" beats "Your plan is adapted".
5. **Concrete numbers > vague.** "14-day plan" beats "fast results".
6. **Match the user's words.** If they said "lose weight", don't pivot to "transform your body".
7. **No jargon.** If your mom needs it explained, rewrite.
8. **Localizable.** Avoid puns and cultural references that won't translate.
